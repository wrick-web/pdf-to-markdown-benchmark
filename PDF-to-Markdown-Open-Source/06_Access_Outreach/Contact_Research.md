# Contact Research

Verified, legitimate contact channels for the organizations behind the
Tier A/B tools in `Tool_Landscape.md`. No guessed email addresses — every
contact below was checked against the organization's own published
material (GitHub README/MAINTAINERS file, official blog post, or a public
statement from a named maintainer), not a scraped-email lookup. Confidence
level is stated for each; anything below HIGH/MEDIUM-HIGH was **not**
used to send an email — see `Outreach_Tracker.md` for what was actually
sent.
