## **Reported fault**

Intermittent baseline noise on channel 2, reported by the Partnership on 21 January. The analyser had
been running without attention since the annual service in January 2025.

## **Work carried out**

The flow cell was removed, stripped and cleaned, and the pump tubing set replaced. The optical window
was found to be lightly fouled on the inner face; it was cleaned rather than replaced, and should be
inspected again at the next visit. The analyser was recalibrated against the two-point standards and left
running on channel 2 for an hour before the site was handed back. The completed worksheet is bound in
as page 2 of this report.
